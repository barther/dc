**Trip style:** One family unit, one major daytime adventure, with day/night difficulty balanced so nobody gets destroyed. If the day is Level 4, the night is Level 1; if the day is light, we can spend the energy after dark.

## Train

### Preferred departure: Anniston, Alabama

Anniston is the preferred boarding point even though Atlanta is geographically closer. The effort is roughly comparable, and Anniston allows an evening boarding instead of waiting until close to midnight in Atlanta.

That makes the train part of the vacation rather than merely transportation.

### Accommodation

The Crescent's sleeper inventory creates the central quirk of the trip: a standard sleeper room only accommodates two people, so three travelers causes Amtrak's booking engine to require two rooms.

If Mom comes:

**4 people + 2 sleeper rooms = sensible.**

Without Mom:

**3 people + 2 sleeper rooms = expensive unused capacity.**

If possible, request rooms close together/across the hall from one another.

---

## Hotel Strategy

### Preferred area: L'Enfant Plaza / northern Wharf / Southwest

Do **not** optimize the hotel around Union Station. We use Union Station twice; we use Washington for seven days.

L'Enfant Plaza is unusually well matched to this itinerary:
- Very convenient to Air & Space
- Easy access to Natural History and the National Mall
- Excellent for the Washington Monument
- Better than most central locations for the Tidal Basin / Jefferson area
- Excellent Metro connectivity
- Direct Blue Line access toward Arlington
- Short Uber/Lyft bailout from most of the major memorial areas

### Hotels to compare

Primary candidate:
- **Hilton Washington DC National Mall The Wharf**

Control candidate:
- **Grand Hyatt Washington / Penn Quarter**

Also compare any genuinely strong value in the L'Enfant / Southwest / northern Wharf box.

### Hotel selection criteria

In order of importance:
1. Location against **this itinerary**
2. Comfortable rooms for a seven-night stay
3. Excellent Metro access
4. Restaurants/food nearby
5. Easy Uber/Lyft returns when the family is cooked
6. Total price for **2 rooms × 7 nights**

Parking is irrelevant. We are not bringing a car to DC.

# Mom + Dog Problem

If Mom comes, the remaining logistical issue is her elderly dog. The dog is **not** coming to Washington; the trip would be too much for her.

Preferred solutions, in order:

1. **Trusted person stays at Mom's house with the dog** — least disruption to an elderly dog's routine.
2. **Dog stays with a familiar friend or relative** she already knows.
3. **Professional overnight house sitter** — likely the best paid solution because the dog remains in her normal environment.
4. **Quiet / senior-oriented or veterinary boarding** with appropriate medication, bathroom, mobility, and overnight-care capability.
5. **Conventional kennel boarding** only if the dog already tolerates boarding well.

If using a house sitter, arrange a few visits and ideally a trial overnight before November so the first experiment is not a seven-night vacation.
