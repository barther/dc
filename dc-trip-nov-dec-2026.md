## Lodging — decision pending, leaning L'Enfant/Wharf

**Leading candidate:** Hilton Washington DC National Mall (The Wharf), L'Enfant Plaza
- Walk to Air & Space, Natural History, Washington Monument
- Closest option to Tidal Basin/Jefferson
- L'Enfant Plaza Metro = major interchange (Blue/Orange/Silver/Green/Yellow) — direct one-seat ride to Arlington Cemetery
- Sample off-peak listing rate seen: ~$155–225/night; breakfast ~$18–25/person/day extra; valet parking $65/day if needed

**Control candidate:** Grand Hyatt Washington, Penn Quarter/Metro Center
- More central to Archives, Smithsonians, White House; livelier neighborhood
- Slightly worse for Tidal Basin and Arlington (one Metro transfer)

**To do:** pull live rates for the confirmed 11/29–12/6 dates (2 rooms) from both properties, plus any other genuinely competitive SW/Wharf options, before booking. At 2 rooms × 7 nights, a $30/night difference is real money — don't decide on geography alone.

The two brutal cold-exposure nights (12/2, and Christmas night 12/5) are each preceded by the two easiest daytime outings (Archives, Washington Monument/market day). No day asks the family to rally twice.

**Emergency rule:** Cold is allowed to shorten an outing. It isn't allowed to ruin the vacation. If it's 28° and windy at Korea, Uber XL → heat, and reshuffle rather than push through. Note: unlike the earlier 12-day draft, this 7-night version has **no dedicated buffer day** inside the DC stretch — if something needs to be cut short or re-done, it has to come out of an already-scheduled slot rather than a free day.
