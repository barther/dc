I.  Getting there

The train is the trip.

No airport. No rental car. We board the Amtrak Crescent in Anniston after Bart gets off work, settle into two sleeper rooms across the hall from each other, sleep through Georgia and the Carolinas, and wake up in Virginia. Sunday afternoon we roll into Union Station, three blocks from the Capitol.

  • ✦ Evening boarding. Anniston lets us leave at a civilized hour instead of waiting up past midnight in Atlanta.
  • ✦ Home base on the Mall. We're aiming for L'Enfant Plaza, a short walk from Air & Space, Natural History, and the Washington Monument, with a one-seat Metro ride to Arlington.

II.  Nine days, one big thing a day

The week.

Every day gets one real adventure, and the day and night are balanced so nobody gets wrecked. Big museum day? Easy night. Easy afternoon? That's when we go out and see the memorials glow. easy  ·  big day

    Bart clocks out at 2:00, we load up, drive to Anniston, eat, and climb onto the Crescent. Nothing to accomplish tonight except finding the bunks and watching Alabama slide by in the dark.

    NightThe train is the activity

    DayAir & Space Museum

  4. TueDec 1

    Capitol Hill.

    Morning tour inside the U.S. Capitol, under the dome. Lunch. Then across the street to the Library of Congress, where the Great Hall of the Jefferson Building is the single most beautiful room in the country. Argue with us after you've seen it.

    DayCapitol + Library of Congress

  5. WedDec 2

    A short daytime visit to the National Archives to stand in front of the Declaration of Independence, the Constitution, and the Bill of Rights. The real ones. Then back to the hotel to warm up and rest, because tonight is the one we'll talk about for years.

    Reach the Vietnam Wall at dusk while the names are still easy to read. Then let it get dark: World War II Memorial, up the steps to Lincoln, and finally the Korean War Memorial, where the statues come alive under the lights. Bundle up. Hot chocolate after.

    DayNational Archives
    NightWWII → Vietnam → Lincoln → Korea
  6. ThuDec 3

    Dinosaurs. The Hope Diamond. The elephant in the rotunda. The ocean hall, the mammals, the giant squid. Sam, this is your day. We stay until everyone has seen the thing they came for.

    DayNatural History Museum
    NightDinner, nothing scheduled
  7. FriDec 4

    Arlington.

    One Metro ride across the river to Arlington National Cemetery. The Tomb of the Unknown Soldier and the Changing of the Guard, which we build the whole day around. President Kennedy's gravesite and the eternal flame. Arlington House on the hill, looking back over the whole city. Quiet, cold, and unforgettable.

    DayArlington National Cemetery
    NightDinner, nothing scheduled

    After dark: the White House, the Ellipse, and the National Christmas Tree with all fifty-six state and territory trees around it. This night isn't for learning anything. It's for lights, cocoa, and seasonal nonsense.

IV.  How we keep it fun

House rules.

Art. ICold can shorten an outing. It can't ruin the vacation.

If it's 28° and windy at the Korean War Memorial, we call an Uber XL, get warm, and reshuffle. Nobody pushes through misery to check a box.

Art. IIThis is not a death march.

One big thing a day. Museum closing time is not a challenge to be defeated. When the family is full, we leave, and the day still counts as a win.

Art. IIISpend the twenty bucks.

Metro when it's convenient, walking when the walk is nice, and a car the second it saves a thousand cold steps at the end of the day. We're not here to win a transit efficiency contest.

Art. IVWe stay together.

One family unit, the whole week. Every day has something worth doing, and the difficulty is budgeted across the whole day so all four of us can do all of it.
