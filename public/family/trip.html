How this page works

  1. The bracket sets the order. Everyone fills one. Every finished ballot is a ranking, the family's average is the order, and the schedule follows it.
  2. One Big thing a day. A Big day gets an Easy night. Never two Bigs in one day. The planner won't let you.
  3. Votes aren't edits. Your vote is your opinion. Any must-do protects a thing; if everyone skips it, it's off.
  4. Ideas stay ideas until someone asks. The planner schedules the must-see things on its own. Extras need a vote.
  5. Nothing consequential lands silently. If a change costs a day or a must-see thing, you'll see the cost first.
